# 0.0.24 plan: Noctiluca's video

**Status:** started on 2026-10-25 as a list of what 0.0.23 hands on. It is
filled in once 0.0.23's cohort is tagged.

## Handed on

### 1. Video in Noctiluca: noctiluca, embed

The maintainer's call on 2026-10-25: video in 0.0.24, after 0.0.23's
images. This is phase 0.4 of the [design note](../design/browser.md).

- **A playing video plays in its region.** Chrome's screencast is cropped
  to that region, only while the video plays.
- **Controls:** play, pause, seek and mute, both from the media controls
  and from keys. Sound already plays with `--audio` in 0.0.22.
- **A frame budget per region.** Each region's frame rate comes from a
  total byte budget. The proof of concept's 8 fps is a ceiling, not a
  target.
- **Stepping down on slow terminals:** Kitty, then iTerm2, then Sixel,
  then quadrant or half-block art. Over SSH, video starts as character
  art.
- **Tests:**
  - the design note's `motion.webm` fixture plays, pauses and seeks;
  - the region's frame rate stays under its cap, counted in bytes over two
    seconds;
  - over a slow simulated terminal, it steps down to character art.

It needs 0.0.23's frame budget and render thread from the
[rendering plan](../design/render-performance.md).
