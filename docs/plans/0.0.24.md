## Waiting on the maintainer

### 1. The marketplace's official keys: marketplace, cli

`rs-rich-marketplace` trusts the keys of a marketplace's `keys-1.json`,
but the official ones are not compiled in yet
([the marketplace note](../design/marketplace.md)): until then a client
names its keys with `RICH_MARKETPLACE_KEYS`. The maintainer makes the two
Ed25519 key pairs (main and backup) and records them in the marketplace
repository as `keys/keys-1.json`; the crate then compiles them in, and
`rich plugin` trusts the official marketplace with no setting.
