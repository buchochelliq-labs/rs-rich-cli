Noctiluca scintillans is a species capable of managing its buoyancy by regulating the intracellular ion concentration. │
To rise, the concentration of potassium will increase and to fall, it will use heavier elements such as calcium or     │
magnesium.[2]                                                                                                          │

N. scintillans has an important place in the pelagic food chain.[2] N. scintillans is preyed upon by many copepods     │
such as Calanus sp., Temora sp. and Acartia sp., chaetognaths and hydromedusae.[7] Because of their excessive          │
proliferation, they attract many predators due to their very dense aggregations and frequent bioluminescence in this   │
phase of their life.[7]                                                                                                │
