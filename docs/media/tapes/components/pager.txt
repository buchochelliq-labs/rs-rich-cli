   1 # orbit
   2
   3 A tiny service, here so the documentation's recordings have something to
   4 page through, pick from and search. Nothing in it runs.
   5
   6 ## What it does
   7
   8 orbit watches a queue, hands each job to a worker and reports progress in the
   9 terminal. Workers are plain functions; the scheduler decides how many run at
  10 once, retries a job that fails, and gives up after three tries.
  11
  12 ## Configuration
  13
  14 | Setting | Default | Meaning |
  15 |---|---|---|
  16 | `workers` | 4 | Jobs that run at once |
  17 | `retries` | 3 | Tries before a job fails |
  18 | `port` | 8080 | Where the status page listens |
