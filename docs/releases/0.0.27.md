# 0.0.27 cohort: Noctiluca's video smooth, the browser recoverable

![Memo, intuiTUIve's elephant, in the cartoon look](../assets/intuituive/mascot/memo-cartoon.svg){ .tui-mascot width="128" }
**Published on 2026-11-18**, every crate and `rs-rich` 0.0.15 on PyPI by
one release run on rab v2.1.2. See [Publication](#publication).

0.0.27 is the last point release before 0.1.0, the API review
([the 1.0.0 plan](../plans/1.0.0.md), stage 3). It fixes what the 0.0.26
real-site check found and three things the maintainer saw: video that
jitters and flickers, Amazon still not right, and "the browser has gone".
[The 0.0.27 plan](../plans/0.0.27.md) lists every item, and
[the findings](../design/noctiluca-0027-findings.md) give their causes.

- **Smooth video in Noctiluca.** Every frame interval on the watch-page
  fixture, with a release build, at 120×40:

  | Protocol | p50 / p95 / max (ms) | Gaps over 120 ms |
  |---|---|---|
  | Kitty | 33.8 / 44.9 / 82.9 | 0 |
  | iTerm2 | 33.5 / 45.3 / 145.6 | 2 |
  | Sixel | 34.0 / 43.4 / 120.2 | 1 |
  | Half blocks | 33.6 / 45.9 / 74.8 | 0 |

  The debug build's Kitty p95 before the fixes was 131 ms. No frame blanks
  its region in any protocol. The changes:
  - Kitty uploads a frame's image before the cells that name it;
  - an opaque live picture in iTerm2 and Sixel is drawn over its cells,
    with no half blocks and no erase under it;
  - readings of the page run on a thread of their own;
  - a playing video stays inside Chrome's view while Noctiluca scrolls;
  - frames are paced by the video's own clock, and dropped in one place.
- **The browser recovers, and says why once.**
  - When Chrome exits or its connection ends, the status line says so,
    with the cause and the key that restarts it, instead of a toast for
    every command.
  - Reload starts another browser and keeps the tab's history.
  - A crashed renderer fails the waiting calls at once and offers a
    reload, where each call waited 30 seconds.
  - The tab's periodic calls wait at most 3 s, and a reading at most 10 s.
- **Shops and chat** (all generic, no per-site code):
  - a native rating replaces the stars' picture (`★★★★½ 4.5`);
  - a grid that snaps to fewer columns pushes what follows it down;
  - three new adaptations, each on by default and each one that can be
    switched off: `badge-text` (a chat badge reads as its alt text),
    `one-row-bars` (a cookie banner is one row with its buttons) and
    `fold-labels` (`Sponsored · Tidewell 1.7 L …`).
- **What 0.0.26 moved on:**
  - #889: the media keys never pick a source-less `<video>`;
  - #890: below;
  - #891 and #892: a live chat log keeps taking lines, and `{` holds what
    is showing;
  - #884: Sixel and iTerm2 no longer encode a picture again when it only
    moved;
  - layouts L2, L6 and L8 of [the YouTube audit](../design/noctiluca-youtube-perf.md);
  - stacked toasts no longer overlap.
- **#890: a live stream's video no longer freezes.** On a busy Twitch
  channel the player's overlay frame (its extensions layer, an
  `<iframe>` as big as the video) hid the video from the view, and the
  screencast stopped for good while the status said `PLAYING`. A frame
  now covers nothing unless its own box is opaque, it lies over the
  video rather than the poster the video hides, and a video counts as on
  screen by the rows it was placed on. On xQc's channel frames ran for
  the whole 80 s check at about 20 a second, where they stopped after 8
  to 17 s. A region still left without frames for two seconds has its
  screencast restarted, and the status says `STALLED` meanwhile.
- **intuiTUIve's widgets for clicks, notices, steps and toasts:**
  - clickable `[@click=…]` markup spans;
  - `notice_bar`, `steps`, `chip` and `tag_input`, `accordion`, `pager`
    and `range_slider`;
  - toasts with buttons, a toast history and `Level::Success`;
  - Success and Warning buttons, and a Bell command (off by default);
  - five more pseudo-classes.

  Streaming Markdown handles loose lists and reference links defined in
  another block (#797). Two new tapes show them: `kit` and `answer`.
- **Cornacai:**
  - an agent installed with npm starts on Windows (`PATHEXT`);
  - `claude-agent-acp`, the renamed Claude adapter, is found first;
  - a failed or stalled start quotes the agent's own error;
  - the plan is intuiTUIve's `steps`, read to a screen reader as a list.
- **Releases:** the old path is retired. `RELEASES.toml`,
  `release_cohort.py` and `pypi-release.yml` are gone, and rab's `rab.yml`
  lists the packages.

A default build of `rs-rich` still renders exactly like upstream `rich`
15.0.0.

## Migration

The [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/buchochelliq-labs/rs-rich-cli/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
has each breaking change, and `api/semver-breaks.toml` lists them with
their reasons:

- **rs-rich-embed:** `PageState` gains `crashed`. A struct literal adds it
  or uses `..Default::default()`.
- **noctiluca:** `Playback` gains `live` and `empty` (#889), and `Rule`
  gains `BadgeText`, `OneRowBars` and `FoldLabels`, so an exhaustive
  `match` on it needs three arms.
- **rs-rich-intuituive:** `Level` gains `Success` and is now
  `#[non_exhaustive]`: a `match` on it outside the crate adds a `_` arm.
- **intuiTUIve's toasts:** a click anywhere on a toast now dismisses it.
  A test that clicks where a toast is shown dismisses the toast first.

### Noctiluca on real sites

Walked on 2026-11-18 at 120×40, once with `--images kitty` and once with
`--images ansi`, with a release build and headless Chromium 153 in a PTY
under a capturing terminal, as in 0.0.26. **Nothing found blocks the
tag.** #890 reproduced on the first pass and is fixed in this release;
the busy channel was checked again on the fixed build.

| Site | Result |
|---|---|
| BBC News, front page and an article | As in 0.0.26: pictures in place, captions from alt text, reading order, reader mode and long-page scrolling worked. The reader view still starts with the page's header |
| A video page (Wikimedia Commons' *Big Buck Bunny*) | The poster, the `[ Play video ]` button and the player dialog worked. The dialog's video started in one run of three: in the other two it stayed at 0:00 and `p` did nothing. Chrome alone, with no Noctiluca, shows the same: after the page's own Play button the video sits at `readyState` 2 near 4 s and its `play()` never settles, so this is Chrome decoding that file headless here, not Noctiluca. ## Not in this release

- **Chrome's user agent** still says `HeadlessChrome`: it is the
  maintainer's call, and is unchanged.
- **Busy live pages** still pause for 1 to 2.5 s now and then (7 to 12
  times in 80 s on xQc's channel); the frames come back by themselves.
  Their cause is not traced yet.
- **Chat badges** read as their names, in brackets as Twitch's buttons:
  a long badge name makes a busy chat wordy (`--adapt=-badge-text` shows
  them as pictures).
- **Amazon live:** this network refuses it, so the shop fixes were checked
  on the shop fixture only.
- **To 0.1.x:** #879 to #882, Cornacai's live agent terminal, Textual's
  state-driven stylesheet selectors and long tail, and the community
  plugin (#831).
- **lumis 0.17** waits: it raises rs-rich-lumis's minimum Rust to 1.95.
- **Memo's illustrations** wait for the maintainer's reference pictures.
