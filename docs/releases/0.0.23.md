# 0.0.23 cohort: faster frames, pictures and video in Noctiluca, and the marketplace goes live

![Memo, intuiTUIve's elephant, in the cartoon look](../assets/intuituive/mascot/memo-cartoon.svg){ .tui-mascot width="128" }
**Published on 2026-10-27**, every crate by one release run, with `rs-rich`
0.0.11 on PyPI. See [Publication](#publication).

0.0.23 began as what [0.0.22](0.0.22.md) handed on and grew as the
maintainer brought items forward from later releases. The core finishes
porting `console.py`, the last partial library row, and gets faster while
its bytes stay the same. intuiTUIve paints with fewer bytes and gains what
rs-rich-doom built for itself. Noctiluca draws a page's pictures, GIFs and
video, and was tried on eight real sites. The plugin marketplace is live and
signed, with its own website. The [0.0.23 plan](../plans/0.0.23.md) has
every item; what moves on is in [0.0.24](../plans/0.0.24.md).

- **`console.py` fully ported.** `quiet`, upstream's size rules and setters,
  colour and terminal detection from the environment (`COLORTERM`, `TERM`,
  `TTY_COMPATIBLE`, `FORCE_COLOR`), `on_broken_pipe`, capture, `record=`
  with the text, HTML and SVG exports, `log`, render hooks,
  `set_live`/`clear_live`, `screen()` and `status()`. `Progress.log`
  works, and a nested `Live` draws inside the first display, as in rich
  15.0.0. New golden fixture `console.tsv`. Every library row in
  [PORTING](../PORTING.md) is now complete or a recorded divergence.
- **Themes reach Markdown.** A theme's `markdown.*` styles show in
  `rich -m`, and `--theme-style repr.number=…` is no longer overridden by a
  fixed colour.
- **rich renders faster, byte for byte the same.** Fast paths beside the
  ported code (colour names, character widths, markup tags, style
  normalisation, word wrapping), each pinned by a test, and
  `Segment.text` and `Color.name` as `CompactString`s. A table of markup
  redraws in a third of the time, and drawing a colour swatch as segments
  allocates 111 times a frame, down from 5,871. The goldens, the parity
  tables, the differential fuzzer and a byte compare of 30,081 cases
  against the previous binary all agree.
- **rs-rich-ext's fast path.** `surface::Draw` writes cells straight into a
  `Screen`; the charts, gauges, heatmaps and the other viz renderables
  implement it, and intuiTUIve draws with it by default. Every cell
  changing at 80x24 takes 104 µs, against ratatui's 340.
- **Paint strategies, with `best` the default.** `best` scrolls rows that
  moved together with the terminal's own scrolling and otherwise paints
  cells: a log scrolled one row sends 147 bytes a frame, down from 4,013.
  `App::paint_strategy` and `RICH_PAINT_STRATEGY` choose another, and
  `App::timedemo` replays a script on the driver's clock and reports every
  frame. A benchmark gate keeps `best` within 5% of the best fixed strategy.
- **Long pages and endless feeds.** `scroll_virtual` builds only the items
  near the view, with no row cap, and loads more near the end.
- **rs-rich-doom's core gaps.** Raw Kitty frames, Sixel in a palette of
  one's own, pixel cells in their own colours, a picture fitted in a pixel
  buffer, a worker in lockstep with the app's clock, sound without a sound
  card, a voice mixer and an output that calls a renderer. See
  [the gaps](../design/doom-core-gaps.md).
- **Sound in recordings.** `Set Audio on` in a tape, or `rich record
  --audio`, records what an intuiTUIve app plays: an MP4 gets an AAC track,
  and the other formats a WAV beside them. Off by default.
- **Pictures and video in Noctiluca.** Pictures are drawn where the page
  puts them, in Kitty, iTerm2, Sixel or half blocks; animated GIFs move; a
  playing video plays in its region, within the terminal's byte budget.
  Media controls, a header and status bar after the proof of concept, and
  `about:noctiluca`. See [Media](../guide/noctiluca/media.md).
- **Noctiluca follows live logs** (`role=log`, `aria-live`) and has eleven
  named terminal adaptations, each one switchable
  ([Adaptations](../guide/noctiluca/adaptations.md)). Long pages and feeds
  scroll with no row cap.
- **Noctiluca on real sites.** A [gallery](../guide/noctiluca/real-sites.md)
  of BBC News, Wikipedia, Hacker News, MDN, The Guardian, GitHub, YouTube
  and Twitch. Recording it fixed seven layout bugs. Its own video fixtures
  replace the old garbled clip, and the guide plays Noctiluca's promo.
- **WebP everywhere.** `rich image`, `rich view`, `rich gif`, `--diff`,
  rs-rich-art, micro assets and Noctiluca read WebP, and an animated WebP
  plays like a GIF. Sixel keeps a picture's exact colours when it has 256
  or fewer.
- **The plugin marketplace is live.** Its index is signed, and the official
  keys are compiled in, so `rich plugin` trusts it with no flags. It lists
  the Mermaid, DOT and lumis crates, two official WASM plugins (`textcase`
  and `wordplay`), five themes and three micro packs. It shows downloads,
  ratings and reviews, and `rich plugin search` filters by kind, tier and
  recommendation. Installed themes work with `--theme`, and installed micro
  packs load on every run. The marketplace has
  [its own site](https://buchochelliq-labs.github.io/rs-rich-marketplace/),
  and the docs have a [Marketplace page](../marketplace.md).
- **HTTPS trusts the system's certificates.** Plugin installs, `rich <url>`
  and the download statistics work behind a TLS-inspecting proxy, follow
  redirects one at a time and keep one timeout for the whole chain.
- **`rich --rst` follows `rich-rst` 2.2.0**: a docutils document tree with
  its transforms, real tables, numbered footnotes and the newer directives.
- **API checks and fuzzing, brought forward from later stages.** Every
  crate's public API is snapshotted and checked in CI with
  `cargo-semver-checks`. Fuzz targets for every parser that takes outside
  input run nightly ([fuzzing](../design/fuzzing.md)); the three bugs they
  found are fixed. The Python package's type stubs pass `stubtest` and
  `mypy --strict`.
- **The docs load nothing from other sites**: fonts are served with the
  site, and the header no longer asks GitHub's API for stars.
- **intuiTUIve against Textual.** [A parity audit](../design/textual-parity.md)
  maps Textual's widgets to intuiTUIve's and files the gaps as issues
  #797 to #815, for the releases before 1.0.0.

A default build of `rs-rich` still renders exactly like upstream `rich`
15.0.0.

## Migration

Breaking changes in 0.0.x crates (the
[CHANGELOG](https://github.com/buchochelliq-labs/rs-rich-cli/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
has each, and `api/semver-breaks.toml` lists them with their reasons):

- **rs-rich: `Segment.text` and `Color.name` are `CompactString`s.** Reads
  compile unchanged. Where a `String` was moved out, write
  `.to_string()` or `.into_string()`; where one is assigned, add
  `.into()`. `Segment::new` takes `impl Into<CompactString>`.
- **rs-rich: the console reads `FORCE_COLOR` and `TTY_COMPATIBLE`**, as rich
  15.0.0 does. For the old `isatty`-only rule, build with
  `.force_terminal(std::io::stdout().is_terminal())`.
- **rs-rich: a broken pipe ends the process with status 1.** To carry on,
  give `ConsoleBuilder::on_broken_pipe` a handler, such as
  `.on_broken_pipe(|console| console.set_quiet(true))`. The `rich` binary
  keeps exiting 0.
- **rs-rich-ext: `rst::parse` returns the document tree** (`rst::Element`);
  `Block`, `Inline` and friends are gone. Rendering with
  `RestructuredText::new` is unchanged.
- **rs-rich-ext: a painter's `Cell` is 12 bytes and `Copy`**; read its text
  with `Cell::text()`, since the `text` field is gone.
- **rs-rich-intuituive:** `FrameStats` gains fields, and `ImageView` and
  `Pixels` come from rs-rich-art. Apps paint with `best` by default; choose
  `PaintStrategy::Cells` for 0.0.22's behaviour.
- **rs-rich-record:** `Recording` has a new `audio` field and `Options` a
  new `audio` switch.
- **rs-rich-ext and rs-rich-micro use zip 9:** rs-rich-ext lists an entry
  whose name cannot be decoded lossily, and rs-rich-micro refuses it.
- **The CLI:** `rich plugin search`'s no-match message names the
  marketplace's site first, and numbers follow `--theme-style
  repr.number` alone.

  `cargo check --workspace --locked`; `check_packages.py` (every crate

### Not in this release

- **AVIF** is not decoded by rs-rich-art; Noctiluca takes AVIF pictures from
  Chrome's pixels. An opt-in `avif` feature is planned for
  [0.0.24](../plans/0.0.24.md).
- **lumis 0.17** waits: it raises rs-rich-lumis's minimum Rust to 1.95.
- **One fuzzer case** remains a known divergence:
  [#769](https://github.com/buchochelliq-labs/rs-rich-cli/issues/769).
- **The signer still pushes with a deploy key**; the move to a GitHub App
  is part of 1.0.0.
